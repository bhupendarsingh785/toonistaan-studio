Robot lagne ke 5 karan

  1. pitch shift se formants khisakte hain (chipmunk/monster) → resample + formant soch
  2. comb-filter reverb (17/23/31 ms) = goonj → reverb band ya smooth tail
  3. line ke andar lambi khamoshi = 'ruk-ruk kar bolna' → 110 ms se lambi gap 65 ms karo
  4. line ke shuru/aakhir ki silence trim nahi = har line ke baad hole
  5. mid patla + high chubhta = hollow/machine awaaz (mid 55–65%, high ≤20%)

Smoothness ke niyam

  1. har line: trim silence (40–60 ms) → andar ki gap 65 ms → 20 ms fade-in
  2. lines ke beech pause 0.35–0.6 s (1.5 s nahi — woh 'hole' lagta hai)
  3. length_scale 0.9–1.2 (1.4+ = bahut dheema, artificial)
  4. micro amplitude dips hatao (woh shabd kaat dete hain)

  4. length_scale 1.4+ kar diya → ruk-ruk kar bolna
  5. line ke andar ki 1 s+ gaps compress nahi kiye
