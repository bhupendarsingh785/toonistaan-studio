## Kaise bhejna hai
- Sirf **mp3 ya wav** bhejo, ek saath 10-20 files bhi chalengi.
- Naam wahi rakho jo upar likha hai (`door_open_wood_1.mp3`) — isse main seedha pehchan lunga.
- Jo nahi mile, chhod do — main Freesound/Mixkit se le lunga.

## Abhi kya system ke paas hai
- **Library (aapke bheje)**: 21 sounds — darwaza (6), kadam (4), horror (8), bheed (2), jungle (1)
- **Freesound** (asli, key lagi hai) — jo library me nahi mila, wahan se
- **Mixkit** (free) — backup
- **Apne banaye**: 19 synth (cooker, chai, chaabi, jhaadu, matka, pankha waghera)
