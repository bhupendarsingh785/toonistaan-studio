INSAN KA BEHAVIOUR — POORA NAKSHA
========================================

Ye file batati hai ki insan ke behaviour ke kitne hisse hote hain aur unke liye kya seekhna hai.

1. AWAAZ (bolna)
  - bolne ki raftaar (pace) — 120-160 shabd/minute normal
  - sur (pitch) — mard 85-180 Hz, aurat 165-255 Hz, bachcha 250-400 Hz
  - utraar-chadhav (intonation) — sawal par aakhir me upar, khabar me neeche
  - thehraav (pause) — 0.3-0.7s saans, 0.8-1.2s soch
  - zordaar-shabd (stress) — jis shabd par zor, wahi matlab
  - surili leh (prosody) — khush, udaas, gussa, pyaar sabki alag leh

2. HASNA
  - khilkhilana (bachcha), thahaka (bada aadmi), dheemi hansi (sharm)
  - hansi ke beech saans lena — asli hansi me 4-6 'ha' phir saans
  - majboori ki hansi (awkward) — chhoti, bina saans

3. RONA / DUKH
  - sookhi rona (aansu bina), phoot padna, sisakna
  - aawaz tutna — gale me bhaari pan
  - saans ka kaanpna — rone me saans tut-tut kar aati hai

4. GUSSA / DARR
  - aawaz chadhti hai, raftaar badhti hai, saans tez
  - darr me aawaz dheemi aur kaanpti hai
  - cheekh — alag hai gusse ki cheekh aur darr ki cheekh

5. BAAT-CHEET KA TAREEKA (conversation)
  - baari-baari bolna (turn-taking) — 200ms ke andar jawab
  - suniye-phir-boliye (backchannel) — 'haan', 'hmm', 'achha'
  - tokna (interruption) — kabhi pyaar se, kabhi bezaar se
  - sawal-jawab ka jod — jawab me sawal ka shabd dohrana

6. SHAREER KI BAAT (nonverbal)
  - muskuraye bina bolna — aawaz me hi muskaan aati hai
  - haath, aankh, sar hilana — aawaz ke saath
  - doori — paas ka insan dheeme, door ka uncha bolta hai

7. RISHTE KE HISAB SE
  - maa-bachcha, dost-dost, ustaad-chhatra, boss-naukar, ajnabi
  - har rishte me alag shabd, alag thehraav, alag pyaar

8. UMAR KE HISAB SE
  - bachcha (2-6), bada bachcha (7-12), kishor (13-19), jawan, aadhi umar, buzurg (60+)
  - har umar me saans, raftaar, sur alag

9. JAGAH KE HISAB SE
  - ghar (aaram se), bazaar (uncha), mandir (dheema), aspataal (fis fis)
  - har jagah ki apni awaaz aur thehraav

10. BHAV (emotion) — 8 buniyaadi
  - khushi, udaasi, gussa, darr, hairani, ghin, pyaar, sharm
  - in sabke saath awaaz ki speed, sur aur saans badalte hain
